# Remotion cursor click

Use this effect to make a static hold or interface recording read as an intentional product interaction.

- Move the pointer to the real interactive target with a restrained ease-out curve; do not make it wander.
- Fade the pointer in shortly before travel and out after the result has had time to register.
- At the click, briefly scale the pointer down, then restore it. Send a small ring outward and fade it within roughly 22 frames at 30fps.
- Pair the visual click with one short click SFX only if the project includes audio. Align its transient to the visual click.
- Keep the pointer over the recording layer and below explanatory annotations. Do not use it where a real captured pointer is already clear.
